Deposits

Dongle orders need a deposit before we buy and modify the dongle for you.

  • Full refund if we cannot complete your order, for example if your car or head unit version is not supported.
  • Full refund if you cancel before we have bought the dongle for your order.
  • Non-refundable if you cancel after the dongle has been bought or the modification has started, because the part has been built for your order.

Dongle warranty

Each dongle is covered for 14 days from handover against defects. If it fails because of a defect, we will repair or replace it at no charge.

Not covered: physical or water damage, a dongle that has been opened, or firmware changed by anyone other than JUJI-ATELIER.

Advanced Sideload

  • If the apps cannot be installed on a supported head unit, you pay nothing.
  • Once the apps are installed and tested with you, the service fee is non-refundable.
  • If the installed apps stop working within 14 days and you have not changed or added anything, we will reinstall them free of charge.

Custom modifications

Wiring, custom RGB DRL and headlamp work is quoted per job before we start.

  • A deposit may be needed for parts. It is fully refundable if we cancel, or if you cancel before the parts are bought.
  • Once parts have been bought or made for your car, the deposit is non-refundable.
  • Our workmanship is covered for 30 days after handover. If a wiring or installation fault appears, we fix it at no charge. Parts follow the supplier's warranty.
  • Not covered: accident damage, water damage from other causes, or later changes made by someone else.
